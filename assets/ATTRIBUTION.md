OpenMoji replacement assets

Source: https://openmoji.org/
License: CC BY-SA 4.0

The SVG files in `assets/openmoji/` were downloaded from the Iconify OpenMoji API and are used as open-licensed substitute assets where the original Stimulation Clicker/source/brand artwork is unavailable or not appropriate to copy directly.

Added substitute categories:
- Original/brand video and app artwork: replaced with same-category OpenMoji icons.
- Loot boxes, powerups, notification bubbles, ads, shop cosmetics, skywriter plane, meditation, hydraulic press, achievement toasts, and the oversized custom cursor: replaced with OpenMoji icons from the same visual category.
